# Admin Polymorph: turning a character into another race

Staff can turn their own character, or an online player's, into any race of
the load order from the Personal Menu's Admin > Polymorph sub-tab, and turn it
back with Revert. It is a staff tool and it may crash games: many races were
never meant to carry a player, so the known crash risks are refused, every
transform and revert is logged, and the original look always comes back on its
own when the character logs out, crashes or the server restarts.

- Race list: `skymp5-server/ts/systems/raceCatalog.ts` (reads every RACE, HDPT and KYWD record of the load order once, in the background)
- Transform and revert: `skymp5-server/ts/systems/polymorph.ts`, wired into `adminSystem.ts` (actions `raceList`, `polymorph`, `polymorphRevert`, cap `polymorph`)
- Client: `skymp5-client/src/services/services/polymorphService.ts` (switches the local skeleton, keeps weapons sheathed where a draw would crash, sends a creature's attacks, fits the camera), `adminMenuService.ts` (passthrough)
- Front: `skymp5-front/src/features/adminPanel/polymorphTab.tsx`
- Character record: `private.polymorph` (the original appearance, the gear worn before a creature form, the race, when and by whom) and the indexed marker `private.indexed.polymorph` so a restart finds every character left transformed
- Test: `node skymp5-server/tools/test-polymorph.js`

---

## How the transform works

A player's race lives in the `appearance` property. The server swaps its
`raceId` (and the head, see below) and stores the original first, only on the
first transform, so a second transform from a wolf into a troll still reverts
to the character's own race.

- **Other players** rebuild the character's copy from the new appearance:
  FormView makes a new NPC base with the new race and spawns it again, so the
  new skeleton and behaviour graph load like any creature's.
- **The transformed player** gets a `polymorph` packet. The appearance apply
  alone only swaps the race of the player's base and never reloads the
  skeleton, so PolymorphService calls `Actor.SetRace` (the vanilla werewolf
  transform does the same), waits 1.5 s for the 3D, applies the server's look
  again and re-syncs the race abilities. A mounted player is dismounted first.
- **Server side**, everything that reads the appearance race follows it while
  transformed: base health, magicka and stamina from the race record, the race
  spells in the spawn packet, unarmed damage and reach. The character's
  roleplay race (`private.rp`) never changes.

### Head and face

| Target race | Head |
|---|---|
| the vampire form of the character's own race, or back from it | the character's own face, hair, tints and morphs |
| another FaceGen race (playable, vampire, Dremora, Elder...) | the race's default head parts (with their extra parts) and default face texture; morphs, presets and tints reset, skin and hair colour kept |
| a race without FaceGen (creatures, Falmer, Giant, Riekling) | no head parts, tints or colours, as the character creator sends for those races |

The name always follows the current appearance, so a `/mask` made while
transformed stays after Revert.

### Gear

A creature form takes the gear off: the server blanks the `equipment` other
players see before the new body goes out (a weapon on a skeleton without its
weapon nodes is the kind of crash the game has shown before), and the client
unequips everything, forces third person (creature skeletons have no first
person body) and takes off anything put on while in that form. Until Revert
its equipment reports leave worn gear out, so an item put on in that form never
reaches the other players' copies even for the one update before it comes off
(`SendInputsService: equipment report #N in a creature form: M worn item(s) left out`).
The worn items are stored with the original look. Revert puts back every stored worn item the
character still carries, on the client at once and in the server's
`equipment` 1.5 s later, after the other players' copies rebuilt the body.
Playable, vampire and other humanoid races keep the gear.

### Weapons and attacks

On a weapon draw the engine shows the player's shield or torch: the
`weaponDraw` animation event runs `RightHandWeaponDrawHandler`, which for the
player alone looks up the Shield Biped Object of the base's race and indexes
the biped slots with it. A race without one (`-1` in the RACE DATA: 101 of the
134 creature races and both Dremora races on the test load order) makes it
read the slot before the array, which is whatever memory lies in front of it.
Both crashes of 2026-10-01 (Riekling, Dragon Priest) are that read
(`SkyrimSE.exe+072ED84`/`+072ECFA`, function 40419, `RBP` -1), and nearly
every creature behaviour graph carries the `weaponDraw` event. So:

- The catalog reads the Shield Biped Object. A form of a race without one has
  the fighting controls off (`noDraw` in the packet): no weapon, spell or fist
  is drawn in it. PolymorphService checks the lock every update, because other
  services switch the fighting controls back on, and a weapon drawn at the
  moment of the transform is put away before the race switches. A draw still
  under way counts as drawn (the graph's `IsEquipping`), and until the weapon
  is away RemoteServer holds the new look back too, so the base keeps the race
  with a shield slot while a `weaponDraw` event can still arrive
  (`PolymorphService: polymorph: a weapon is drawn or being drawn, the race
  switch and the look wait until it is away`). This relies on the appearance
  update and the `polymorph` packet arriving in the same network tick, as the
  server sends them together.
- A creature under that lock attacks on the attack key (the control
  `Right Attack/Block`, the left mouse button by default): the client sends
  the next attack event of the race's attack data (`ATKE`, bashes left out,
  power attacks only for a race with nothing else, eight at most) to the
  graph, one every 0.9 s at most. No attack is sent while the player is
  bound, carried, carrying someone, downed, in an execution pose or an action
  lock (RestraintService's `isPoseLocked`), while chat or a menu has the
  input, or while the Dialogue, Magic, Favorites, Message Box or Sleep/Wait
  menu is open, and a click that waited more than 0.2 s behind a pausing menu
  is dropped. The hit is the engine's own unarmed hit
  (source `1f4`), which the server accepts for any race and rates with the
  race's unarmed damage. Dragon priests, seekers, chickens and hares have no
  attack data and do not attack.
- A Dremora form keeps its gear but cannot draw it; the admin's reply says so.
- A creature of a race with a shield biped object (Draugr, Falmer, skeletons,
  spriggans, gargoyles, the frost atronach) draws and attacks the engine's
  way. Only the graphs with an idle for the attack action answer the attack
  key: Draugr, Falmer and gargoyles on both hands, spriggans and the frost
  atronach on the left hand (block key) only.

A native guard on that handler would lift the lock; none is written yet.

When a form ends (Revert, or a switch to a form that needs fewer locks) the
fighting and camera-switch controls are handed back and RestraintService
writes its own locks and pose again at once, so a player reverted while bound,
carried or downed stays unable to fight or to leave third person.

### Camera

The third person camera pivots at the root of the player's 3D plus the height
of the skeleton's `Camera3rd [Cam3]` node. Only the playable skeletons, the
vampire lord, the werewolf and the storm atronach have that node, so a creature
form's pivot sat at its feet. 1.5 s after the switch PolymorphService looks for
the node; without it, it measures the height of the skeleton's head node (a
list of the vanilla head node names), adds it to `fOverShoulderPosZ` and
`fOverShoulderCombatPosZ`, scales `fVanityModeMinDist` and
`fVanityModeMaxDist` by height / 120 (1 to 4) for a tall body and calls
`UpdateThirdPerson`. The four settings go back before the next race switch, on
Revert and on a new spawn. The switch to first person is off in a creature
form.

### Sex

A race with a skeleton for one sex only (Alduin, the undead dragon, the
Essence of Magicka, the swarms) transforms a character of the other sex into
the sex it has. Revert restores the original sex.

## The race list

Built from the load order, the last override of each record winning. Deleted
and child races are left out. Groups:

- **Playable**: the playable flag.
- **Vampire**: carries the `Vampire` keyword and its morph race is playable (NordRaceVampire and the other nine, Winterhold Restored's Hel).
- **Other people**: FaceGen or `ActorTypeNPC` races that are neither (Dremora, Elder, Snow Elf, Golden Saint...).
- **Creature**: everything else.

A race needs both a skeleton (`ANAM`) and a behaviour graph (`MODL` under
`NAM3`) for at least one sex; without them the row is greyed and the server
refuses it with "has no skeleton or behaviour graph". Known crash risks show in
red, greyed like those, and the server refuses them for every tier ("is marked
as a crash risk (...), refused"):

- placeholder, prop or effect races without a playable body (DefaultRace, TestRace, InvisibleRace, ManakinRace, the rigid skeletons, the fake coffin and the instruments, the wisps, witchlights and swarms, the Essence of Magicka, the ballista)
- flying races (every dragon): players cannot fly them and dragon forms are known to crash
- immobile races and water-only races (slaughterfish)
- the werewolf, werebear and vampire lord beast forms, whose transform quests are not running
- horses, which other players may try to mount

The test load order of 2026-09-30 gives 170 races: 10 playable, 11 vampire,
15 other people and 134 creatures, none without a skeleton and 29 marked and
refused. A race wrongly marked is unmarked in `riskOf` in `raceCatalog.ts`.

## Reverting

Revert on the tab, or on a row of the transformed list under it, puts the
stored appearance back and clears the record. The server also reverts on its
own:

- when the player logs out or their game crashes (the disconnect), so the body left for the logout grace and the next login are the original,
- when the player opens character select from the game,
- at boot, for every character the index still lists (a server crash while transformed),
- on actor assign, as a last catch.

"switch failed" after the actor race means `Actor.SetRace` did not take;
"look not received yet" means the appearance update had not arrived 1.5 s
after the switch (it applies when it lands). The attack line is written for the
first five attacks of a form; "IsAttacking false" means the graph did not take
the event in the state it was in (a creature that only attacks from its combat
stance, which needs the weapon draw this form must not make).

## Rights

The `polymorph` cap, on for every tier by default; `adminTierCaps` can turn it
off per tier (`"gm": { "polymorph": false }`). Every tier may transform any
online player, itself included.
